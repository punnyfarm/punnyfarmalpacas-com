# Punny Farm Website

This repository contains a simple, welcoming webpage for **Punny Farm**.

Punny Farm is a playful, family-style farm experience featuring alpacas, dogs, and cats. The website introduces visitors to the farm's fun spirit and includes mention of the on-site Airbnb where guests can stay and enjoy the animals.

## Purpose

The site is meant to provide an online home for people who are curious about Punny Farm and want to quickly understand:

- What Punny Farm is
- Which animals live there
- The happy, pun-inspired vibe
- That an Airbnb stay is available on the property

## Editing Guidelines

If you update this site, keep the tone:

- Lighthearted
- Friendly and family-oriented
- Positive and pun-inspired

Favor clear language and simple structure so visitors can understand the farm at a glance.
